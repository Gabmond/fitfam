A beginner friendly program to help you Build strength, Grow muscle, and Get fitter.

Ready to build muscle and get stronger? This 4-week plan helps you develop strength, improve your fitness and master bodyweight exercises. Start at your level, stay consistent and gradually challenge yourself as you improve.

PROGRESSION

See how you'll build strength, improve your technique, and gradually challenge yourself over four weeks.

week 1 - LEARN

Learn the movements, practise controlled technique, and establish manageable working sets.

week 2 - BUILD CONSISTENCY

Repeat the movements and gradually increase repetitions when appropriate.

week 3 - PROGRESS

Increase repetitions, resistance, or exercise difficulty only when the current variation can be performed with good technique.

week 4 - CONSOLIDATE

Repeat the exercises, compare performance with Week 1, and record yourimprovements.
